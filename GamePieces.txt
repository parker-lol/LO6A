LO6A - KerPlop Game
Parker Lollini and Eknara Dassanayake

Total unique pieces: 7 (Alien, BlackHole, Meteor, Spaceship, SpaceStation, Star, Wormhole)
Drawable only  
    Meteor implements only the Drawable interface. It acts as a static background object and does not interact with
    the player in any way.
Moveable game pieces
    Spaceship uses a linear movement system to travel in one direction and turn around once it reaches the end of the board.
    Alien satisfies the random motion requirement by using java.util.Random to decide whether to move left or right on each turn.
Static interacting pieces
    BlackHole, Star, Wormhole, and SpaceStation extend GamePiece but do not move. Their interactions are described in more detail below.

Interaction Results Coverage
Each required interactionResult enum is utilized across the 7 game pieces:
    HIT: Alien (same square)
    KILL: BlackHole (distance)
    ADVANCE: Wormhole (same square)
    GET_POINT: Star (same square)
    NONE: Spaceship and SpaceStation

Interaction at a distance
    BlackHole calculates the absolute value of the distance between itself and the player, and triggers KILL if the player is within
    one space of it (landing on or adjacent to it). 
    The three static interacting pieces (Star, WormHole, SpaceStation) require the player to be on their exact location to 
    proccess an interaction.

Levels
    The LevelSetup class configures and populates two distinct levels, which are outlined in the CreateLevel function from lines 22 to 63.
